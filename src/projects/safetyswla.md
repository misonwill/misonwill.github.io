The Health and Safety Council Louisiana is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing cost-effective safety services and training to the local community and businesses in Southwest Louisiana. They offer a comprehensive range of educational resources, including site-specific plant safety orientations, customized badge security systems, and various safety courses.
