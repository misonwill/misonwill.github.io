KOA (Kampgrounds of America) is the world's largest network of privately owned campgrounds, featuring over 500 locations across North America with diverse lodging options and amenities. They aim to connect people to the outdoors and each other through various camping experiences, including RV sites, tent sites, cabins, and glamping accommodations.
